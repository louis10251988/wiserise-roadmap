📱 家庭手机极简规则 (10岁秒懂版) Simple Family Phone Rules (10-Yr-Old Plain English)
💡 写给 13 岁和 14 岁： 这不是惩罚，而是帮你睡好觉、不被手机算法控制的简单家规。（5岁弟弟/妹妹还太小，严禁拥有个人手机）。 💡 For 13 & 14-year-olds: This is not a punishment. It's a simple system to protect your sleep and keep you from getting hooked by algorithms. (5-year-olds do not get personal phones).
🏛️ 全家必须遵守的三条简单铁律 The 3 Big Rules You Need to Know
🎯 一句话道理： 就像你不会把“游戏厅投币机”搬到床上睡觉一样，手机也不能带进卧室。 🎯 The Big Idea: You wouldn't put an arcade slot machine right next to your pillow at night. The same goes for your phone.
  • 规则 1：睡觉前手机必须放厨房（卧室严禁放手机） Rule 1: Phones Sleep in the Kitchen (No Phones in Bed)
    • 每晚 9:00（周末 10:00），把手机插在厨房中央充电桩上充电。 Every night by 9:00 PM (10:00 PM on weekends), plug your phone into the kitchen charging dock.
    • 卧室床头放一个真正的物理小闹钟，负责叫你起床。 You get a real alarm clock on your nightstand to wake you up in the morning.
    • 为什么： 手机屏幕的蓝光会欺骗你的大脑，让你睡不着、长不高、第二天上课头晕。 Why: Blue screen light tricks your brain into staying awake, ruining your sleep and focus.
  • 规则 2：放学写作业时，手机不能放书桌上 Rule 2: No Phone at the Desk During Homework Time
    • 下午 3:30 到 5:30 写作业期间，手机留在厨房充电。 From 3:30 to 5:30 PM, your phone stays in the kitchen dock.
    • 如果写作业需要查资料、写作文，用带键盘的电脑。 Need to do school research or type an essay? Use a computer with a real keyboard.
    • 为什么： 手机屏幕每闪一次消息弹窗，你刚刚建立的专注力就彻底归零。 Why: Every notification ding resets your brain and makes homework take twice as long.
  • 规则 3：手机里彻底删掉“短视频老虎机” Rule 3: Delete the Video Slot Machines
    • 手机上彻底删掉 TikTok、Instagram Reels、YouTube Shorts。 Delete TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts from your personal phone.
    • 发短信、打电话、听歌（Spotify）、查地图、学校软件全部正常可用。 Texting, calling friends, Spotify music, maps, and school apps stay 100% open.
    • 为什么： 短视频软件是几千个工程师故意设计的诱饵，专门偷走你的时间和精力。 Why: Infinite-scroll videos are engineered like casino games to trap your brain in an endless loop.

10秒记住三句话： Remember in 10 Seconds:

  • 晚上 9 点放厨房充电，卧室只放小闹钟。 Phone goes to kitchen dock at 9:00 PM. Alarm clock in bedroom.
  • 做作业手机不进书房，打字查题用电脑。 Homework is done on a laptop with a keyboard, not on your phone.
  • 短视频删干净，朋友短信随你发。 No endless video feeds; texting and music are always fine.
📅 每天什么时候可以用手机？ When Can I Use My Phone Every Day?
  • 周一到周四（上学日作息）： Monday to Thursday (School Days):
    • 07:00 – 08:30（早晨出门）： 从厨房拿起手机。可以听音乐、看天气、给爸妈发消息。不刷娱乐视频。 7:00 – 8:30 AM (Morning): Grab phone from dock. Listen to music, check weather, message parents. No games/fun videos.
    • 08:30 – 15:30（学校上课）： 手机拉好拉链放书包里。只有紧急情况或放学接送才拿出来。 8:30 AM – 3:30 PM (School): Stays zipped in your backpack. Emergency and pickup calls only.
    • 15:30 – 17:30（作业体能）： 手机放回厨房充电桩。安安静静写作业、运动。严肃查资料用电脑。 3:30 – 5:30 PM (Homework): Phone rests on kitchen dock. Focus on homework and sports. Use computers for schoolwork.
    • 17:30 – 18:30（整理放松）： 可以听歌、做家务、整理房间、出门走走。 5:30 – 6:30 PM (Chores/Break): Music, chores, cleaning your room, going outside.
    • 18:30 – 19:30（全家晚饭）： 手机放厨房。餐桌上大家一起开心吃饭聊天，零屏幕。 6:30 – 7:30 PM (Dinner): Phone in kitchen. Zero phones at the table. Talk with family.
    • 19:30 – 21:00（自由放松时间）： 你的自由时间！打游戏、看长视频、和朋友聊天（黄灯软件限额2小时）。 7:30 – 9:00 PM (Fun Time): Your free window! Play games, watch videos, chat with friends (2-hour limit).
    • 21:00 起（熄灯停泊）： 手机放回厨房充电。上床睡觉，保证 9 小时充足好睡眠。 9:00 PM (Bedtime Dock): Phone plugs into kitchen dock. Sleep time.
  • 周五（放假轻松日）： Friday (Weekend Kickoff):
    • 放学先花一点时间把周五作业写完，周末就没有心理负担。 Finish homework right after school so you have zero stress all weekend.
    • 晚上可以看电影、聚会。手机停泊时间顺延到 晚上 10:00 (22:00)。 Watch movies or hang out. Curfew is extended to 10:00 PM at the kitchen dock.
  • 周六（运动与朋友日）： Saturday (Sports & Friends):
    • 白天多去户外运动、打球、和朋友逛街。 Spend the day outdoors, playing sports, or with friends.
    • 娱乐游戏总时间最多 3 小时。晚上 10:00 (22:00) 放厨房充电。 Total game/fun screen time capped at 3 hours. Kitchen dock at 10:00 PM.
  • 周日（归位重置日）： Sunday (Reset & Ready for School):
    • 下午 5:00 任务关卡： 把周一书包收拾好、校服找好、检查 Canvas 没有欠交作业。 5:00 PM Check: Pack your backpack, lay out school clothes, make sure zero homework is missing.
    • 晚间奖励： 任务过关，晚上 7:30 到 9:00 正常解锁玩手机！没做完就扣除娱乐时间。 Reward: Pass the 5 PM check, and you unlock normal phone fun from 7:30 to 9:00 PM!
    • 晚上 9:00 准时放厨房充电，准备周一精力充沛起床。 Strictly back to 9:00 PM kitchen dock to be ready for school Monday.

  • 🔴 红灯软件：手机上彻底删除（老虎机垃圾） 🔴 Red Tier: Completely Deleted (Addictive Slot Machines)

    • 规则： 个人手机上彻底卸载，电脑浏览器也加上封锁。 Rule: Deleted from phones. Blocked in computer browsers too so they don't follow you to your desk.
⚙️ 手机怎么设置？爸妈 10 分钟搞定指南 How to Set Up the Phone (Parents 10-Min Guide)
🔒 防偷窥第一铁律： 永远不要在孩子面前输入 4 位屏幕时间密码！小孩看一眼反光或手指动作就能猜出来。 🔒 Rule #1 for Parents: NEVER type the 4-digit Parent PIN while your kids are in the room. They will memorize your finger taps instantly.
  • 苹果 iPhone 设置步骤： Apple iPhone Setup Steps:
    • 设密码： 打开“屏幕使用时间” ➔ 开启“锁定设置” ➔ 输一个孩子绝对猜不到的 4 位密码。 Set PIN: Go to Screen Time ➔ Lock Screen Time Settings ➔ Enter a secret 4-digit PIN.
    • 定熄灯时间： 点“停用时间” ➔ 周日至周四设为 21:00 到 07:00，周五周六设为 22:00 到 07:00 ➔ 勾选“阻止设备”。 Set Downtime: Tap Downtime ➔ Set 9:00 PM – 7:00 AM (10:00 PM on weekends) ➔ Check "Block at Downtime".
    • 加始终允许： 点“始终允许” ➔ 勾上电话、短信、地图、音乐、学校软件。 Always Allowed: Add Phone, Messages, Maps, Spotify, Canvas.
    • 定娱乐限额： 点“App 限额” ➔ 选“游戏与娱乐” ➔ 设 2 小时 ➔ 勾选“达到限额时阻止”。 App Limits: Add Limit ➔ Select Games/Entertainment ➔ Set 2 Hours ➔ Check "Block at End of Limit".
    • 禁止乱下载： 点“内容访问限制” ➔ “App Store 购买” ➔ 将“安装 App”设为“需要家长批准”。 Block Downloads: Content Restrictions ➔ Store Purchases ➔ Set "Installing Apps" to Require Parent Approval.
  • 安卓手机 (Google Family Link) 设置步骤： Android (Google Family Link) Setup:
    • 绑定账号： 家长用 Family Link 把孩子 Google 账号连在一起。 Link Account: Connect your teen's Google account to Google Family Link on parent's phone.
    • 就寝锁定： 设成就寝时间（周日至周四 21:00，周末 22:00），时间一到自动锁屏。 Bedtime Lock: Set Bedtime (9:00 PM weekdays, 10:00 PM weekends) to auto-lock the phone.
    • 游戏限额： 游戏和 YouTube 限额 2 小时。 Daily Limits: Set 2-hour daily caps on games and video apps.
    • 下载审批： 开启 Google Play 所有应用下载必须家长点赞同意。 Approval: Require parent approval for every app installation from Google Play.
  • 买齐 3 样便宜好用的硬件工具： 3 Cheap Hardware Tools You Need:
    • 厨房多口排插（$15）： 放在厨房最显眼地方，贴上“手机停泊站”。 Kitchen Charging Hub ($15): A multi-port USB charger right on the kitchen counter.
    • 两个床头闹钟（$10/个）： 两个孩子房间各放一个，彻底废除“手机放床头当闹钟”的借口。 Two Alarm Clocks ($10 each): Put one in each bedroom so they never need a phone to wake up.
    • 带键盘的电脑： 做作业打字查资料用电脑，手机在厨房。电脑浏览器顺手屏蔽 TikTok 网页。 Computer with Keyboard: Homework is typed on a PC. Block TikTok website in the browser too.

    • 爸妈每天只要在 21:01 瞥一眼厨房台面，省心又省力。 Parents spend only 5 seconds glancing at the kitchen counter at 9:01 PM.
📊 做到了有什么好处？犯规了怎么办？ What Happens If... (Clear Cause & Effect)
⚖️ 零吵架原则： 规则提前写好，不用生气、不用骂人，发生了什么就按规矩办。 ⚖️ Zero-Argument Rule: The rules are clear in advance. No yelling, no lectures. Just simple cause and effect.
  • 情况 A：每晚 9:00 按时把手机放厨房充电 Case A: Phone Docked in Kitchen on Time (9:00 PM)
    • 结果：满分！ 爸妈一句唠叨都没有，充分信任你，给你绝对的社交与聊天隐私。 Result: Full Pass! Zero parental nagging, 100% trust, and full privacy for your texting.
  • 情况 B：9:01 手机没放厨房（超时了/忘记了） Case B: Phone Missing from Kitchen at 9:01 PM
    • 结果：次日保管 24 小时。 明天整天手机由爸妈保管。不批评、不吵架，后天恢复正常。 Result: 24-Hour Confiscation. Parents hold the phone tomorrow. No yelling, returns the next day.
  • 情况 C：偷偷把手机带进卧室睡觉 Case C: Sneaking the Phone into the Bedroom Overnight
    • 结果：红线惩罚！没收 48 小时。 卧室是神圣的睡眠领地，必须严格守护。 Result: Red Line Ban! 48-Hour Lock. The bedroom is sacred for sleep and rest only.
  • 情况 D：做作业时间（15:30 – 17:30）偷偷玩手机 Case D: Using Phone During Homework Block (3:30 – 5:30 PM)
    • 结果：取消今晚娱乐。 今晚 19:30 – 21:00 的 2 小时游戏/视频时间全部取消。 Result: Evening Window Forfeit. Tonight's 7:30 – 9:00 PM leisure screen time is canceled.
  • 情况 E：试图偷看或破解爸妈的 4 位管理密码 Case E: Trying to Guess or Shoulder-Surf Parent PIN
    • 结果：改密码 + 停用娱乐 3 天。 密码立即重置，停用黄灯娱乐软件 3 天。 Result: PIN Reset + 3-Day Screen Ban. Passcode changed immediately; games locked for 3 days.
