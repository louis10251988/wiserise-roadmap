People Recruiting — Runbook

When to Use This

You need to hire someone for a restaurant position (server, cook, host, bartender, dishwasher, prep, sushi chef, busser, etc.) at a specific store.

How to Start

Say this in your store's Slack channel:

Need 1 Server

Replace the number and role as needed. Examples: - Need 2 Cooks - Need 1 Host - Need 1 Dishwasher

Only the GM (General Manager) can request hires. If you're not the GM, ask your GM to post it.

What Happens Next (The Full Flow)

Stage 1 — Recruiting (Steps 1–7)

Step 1 — You Post the Request - Post "Need N [role]" in your store channel as a new message - WISE OPS detects your store from the channel automatically (never asks "which store") - Verifies you are the GM

Step 2 — WISE OPS Records the Request - Creates a record in the system with status "recruiting" - Replies in your thread with a confirmation

Step 3 — WISE OPS Checks If You Actually Need This Person - Compares how many people you have now (from 7shifts) vs how many you need (from your Staff Count Sheet) - Formula: MIN = weekly shifts ÷ 4, SAFE = MIN × 1.25, GAP = SAFE − current count - If you're within the gap → auto-approved, pipeline opens - If hiring would put you over the safe number → HR must approve in the thread first. Reply approve or decline.

Step 4 — Job Opens on the Careers Website - WISE OPS confirms a job posting is live on your careers site (e.g., eatatgangnam.com/careers) - Candidates who apply online flow into the system automatically - The pipeline is always open — it never closes

Step 5 — WISE OPS Sends You Candidates - As candidates apply and score above 60, WISE OPS posts a shortlist in your thread - Shortlist is limited to top candidates (gap + 2, max 5) - Two tiers: - 🔥 Call Today (score 80–100) — strong candidate, call same day - 📋 Interview (score 60–79) — passed initial screen, schedule an interview - Each candidate shows: name, score, apply date, email, phone, availability, experience - Ask for detail on any candidate to get personalized interview questions

⭐ GATE 1 — You Pick Who to Hire (Human Decision)

This is your decision. WISE OPS will never auto-hire.

  • Reply in the thread with a number to pick a candidate (e.g., "1" or "Pick 2")
  • Or reply with the candidate's name
  • WISE OPS confirms the pick and moves to Step 7

Step 7 — Hired - WISE OPS updates the record to "hired" - Posts a hire confirmation in your thread - Notifies HR channel and Ops channel - Onboarding begins automatically

Stage 2 — Onboarding (Steps 8–14, Mostly Automatic)

After hire, most of the work is automated. Here's what happens:

Step 8 — 7shifts Account Created (Auto) - WISE OPS creates a 7shifts account for the new hire - 7shifts sends an invite email to the employee - Training courses auto-assigned (New Hire Basics + role-specific course) - HR channel gets a notification

Step 9 — Chowbus + Gusto Accounts Created (Auto) - 7shifts integration auto-creates a Chowbus (POS) account - 7shifts integration auto-creates a Gusto (payroll) account - This takes up to 24 hours (syncs at 5 AM daily) - If stuck beyond 24 hours, HR is notified

Step 10 — WISE OPS Watches and Nudges (Auto) - Monitors setup progress - If something is stuck (e.g., employee hasn't accepted invite), HR is nudged - HR handles stuck accounts — you don't need to chase

Step 11 — I-9 / E-Verify (Auto, Legal Gate) - Gusto handles I-9 and E-Verify automatically - No employee goes Active without I-9 on file — this is a legal requirement - If I-9 is missing, WISE OPS blocks activation and notifies HR

⭐ GATE 2 — Training Checklist (Your Decision)

This is the only human action in Stage 2.

When all 4 setup steps are complete (7shifts, Chowbus, Gusto, I-9), WISE OPS posts a training checklist in your thread:

🎯 Train · [Name] Setup done. Mark days 1–5. 👉 @[GM] reply day 1 done [trainer]

What you do: - After each training day, reply in the thread: - day 1 done Levi (day number + trainer name required) - day 2 done Sarah - day 3 done Mike - etc. - If a training day fails: day 3 fail - After all 5 days: reply pass or fail

Rules: - Trainer name is required for each "done" day - No auto-pass — you must explicitly reply pass - Any fail = employee stays in onboarding - No reply = no pass = stays in onboarding

Step 13 — Employee Activated (Auto) - When all conditions are met (7shifts ✅, Chowbus ✅, Gusto ✅, I-9 ✅, training passed ✅) - WISE OPS activates the employee automatically - Posts activation notice in your thread - Notifies Ops to add to the schedule

Step 14 — Cross-Department Handoff (Auto) - Ops channel notified: position filled → employee active → add to schedule

Walk-In Hire (Bypass Steps 1–6)

If you hired someone face-to-face (walk-in, referral, or internal transfer), you skip the candidate pipeline entirely.

Say this in your store channel:

Hired walk-in John Smith, Server, john@email.com, 555-1234

Or:

Walk-in hire: Host, Sarah Lee

What happens: - WISE OPS detects the store from the channel - Verifies you are the GM - Checks for duplicates (by email and phone) before creating a record - Creates the record with status "hired" directly - Jumps to Step 7 (hire confirmation + onboarding)

If the person already exists in the system, WISE OPS will ask you to confirm before creating a duplicate.

Interview Sheet Photo Upload

If you have a completed paper Interview Sheet (handwritten form from an interview):

  1. Take a photo of the completed sheet
  2. Upload it to your store channel with a message like "onboard this candidate" or "hired this person"
  3. WISE OPS reads the handwritten data (name, email, phone, scores, decision)
  4. If the sheet says "Hire" — treated as a walk-in hire, goes straight to Step 7
  5. Pay rate is auto-filled from the Pay Band Policy if left blank

People Roster Sync — Runbook

When to Use This

You need to know who works at a store, how many active people you have, or whether your team list is accurate.

Common questions this answers: - "Who works here?" - "How many servers do we have?" - "What's our headcount?" - "Is the roster clean?" - "Are there people in 7shifts missing from the system?"

How to Start

Say this in your store's Slack channel:

roster

Or any of these: - headcount - who works here - team list - how many servers do we have - staffing - check 7shifts (to compare Brain vs 7shifts)

WISE OPS detects your store from the channel automatically.

What Happens Next

Step 1 — WISE OPS Pulls Your Roster - Reads the live roster from the system (Brain), not from a spreadsheet - Detects your store from the channel (never asks "which store")

Step 2 — WISE OPS Counts Current Active Staff by Role - Counts only active employees (not hired/in-training, not terminated) - Groups by role: GM, AGM, Floor Manager, Server, Host, Busser, Cook, etc. - Separates out: - Pipeline (hired but not yet active — still in onboarding) - Unknown role (people with "Team Member" or blank position — data issue)

Step 3 — WISE OPS Flags Data Issues Checks for: - Duplicate names (same person twice) - Bad names (email addresses in name field, formatting junk) - Missing or "Team Member" positions (should be a real role like Server, Cook, etc.) - People hired more than 14 days ago but still not active (stuck onboarding) - People active in the system but not in 7shifts (may have left)

Step 4 — WISE OPS Replies in Your Thread

You'll see something like:

📋 Roster — Gangnam Wilmington · 00003

CURRENT (active): GM 1 · Floor 2 · Server 18 · Host 6 · Bus 5
Bodies active: 32  ·  Pipeline hired: 3  ·  Unknown role: 0

✅ Data: Clean

👉 Hire seats: run min-safe-gap · Board: run scheduling

If there are data issues, you'll see:

⚠️ Data: 2 dupes, 1 bad name, 3 unknown role

Step 5 — If You Want Names (Optional)

Ask "show me names" or "who are the servers?" and WISE OPS will post the full name list by role in the thread.

Step 6 — If You Want to Check 7shifts

Say "check 7shifts" and WISE OPS will: - Pull the active 7shifts list for your store - Compare to the Brain roster - Show who's in 7shifts but missing from Brain - Offer to import missing people (with your approval — never auto-imports)

Step 7 — If You Want to Know Who Left

Ask "who left?" or "ghosts?" and WISE OPS will: - Compare Brain active vs 7shifts active - List people who are active in Brain but not in 7shifts (may have left) - Never auto-terminates anyone — just reports for you to confirm

What You'll See When It Works

  1. Roster summary — role counts first, total bodies, pipeline count, unknown role count
  2. Data health — clean ✅ or issues flagged ⚠️
  3. Optional name list — full names by role in the thread
  4. Optional 7shifts compare — missing people table if you asked
  5. Optional drift report — people who may have left (read-only, no auto-fire)

"Team Member" or blank position = data issue Can't do hire math without a real role
