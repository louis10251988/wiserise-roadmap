People & Staffing

Staffing is a capacity problem. Hiring is the response to a proven gap, not a reflex.

人手是容量问题。招聘是对已证明缺口的回应，不是条件反射。

7shifts + Brain roster
GAP FORMULA
GAP = max(0, SAFE − CURRENT)

MIN = weekly shifts ÷ 4
SAFE = MIN × 1.25
CURRENT = live roster

NORMAL STAFFING LOOP
#MoveOwnerGate
1Publish the schedule and identify the actual gapGM—

3Request hire with role and countGMBrain ticket

5Onboard, train, verify, then activateGM + HRTraining pass
