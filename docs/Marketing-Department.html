✅ Design decision · first principles
The job

Make more guests choose Wiserise again.

Marketing is not “make posts.” It is a closed loop: earn trust → create a reason to return → prove what worked → repeat. Ads come last because they only multiply what already works.

The one test / 唯一测试
If we remove this work, does guest demand or repeat revenue get weaker? If not, park it. 如果不会，就先不做。
Six jobs, in order / 六个工作，按顺序
01 · Trust

Be easy to believe

Reviews, hours, menu, photos, and honest replies are correct.

02 · Offer

Give a real reason

Only approved offers with clear economics and a named campaign.

03 · Content

Show the food

Real food, real store, platform-native copy. No invented claims.

04 · Approval

Put a human in charge

GM, Marketing, or Louis approves the right thing before launch.

05 · Publish

Ship without guessing

Humans post social and video. Brain records the campaign state.

06 · Learn

Keep what wins

Measure response, review patterns, repeat visits, and next action.

What “done” means / 什么叫完成

Content is not done when the file exists.

It is done when the human picked a design, the approved facts are visible, the file is saved, and the campaign record is updated.

A review is not done when a draft exists.

It is done when the right approver approves, the reply is published by a human, and the pattern is logged for store coaching.

First-principles filters / 第一性原理筛选
1
Truth before reachWrong hours, menu, photos, or unanswered reviews waste every later effort.
2
Repeat before scaleOne new visit is a transaction. A second and third visit are a system.
3
Proof before opinionKeep a named campaign and measure the response. Do not call a post a win because it looks good.
4
Small test before big spendPaid campaigns need a goal, budget, owner, approval, and measurement plan.

Brand rollout principle / 品牌顺序
Prove once, then copy the system.Start with the approved pilot brand/store. Fix the workflow cheaply. Replicate the proven playbook to the other brands. Do not create a separate system for every store.
