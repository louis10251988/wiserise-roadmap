What good looks like · 什么叫完成

One source of truth

Brain holds live campaign state. Drive holds originals. Slack holds human action and approval.

One accountable next step

A campaign is never “done” without an owner, due time, approval state, and result or UNKNOWN.

Stop rule: Missing fact = MISSING INPUT. Missing result = UNKNOWN. Never fill either gap with a guess.
