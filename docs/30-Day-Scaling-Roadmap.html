Learn the Business Formula
Customers − Costs = Profit. Write: how customers find you, what they buy, what it costs to serve them, what profit remains.客人−成本=利润。写下：客人怎么找到你、买什么、服务成本多少、剩多少利润。

📋 Rules规矩
What keeps this roadmap honest.让这份路线图不跑偏的规矩。
✅ Verified已验证 · 2026-08-03 · Louis
1. Every day produces something real.每天产出真实的东西。 Not a note. Not a plan. A real artifact you can show someone.不是笔记。不是计划。是能给别人看的真实成果。
2. Use real Wiserise data.用真实的Wiserise数据。 Every number comes from Brain, Chowbus, or 7shifts — not guesses.每个数字来自Brain、Chowbus或7shifts——不是猜的。
3. One problem at a time.一次一个问题。 Don't try to fix everything. Find the biggest one. Fix it. Move on.不要什么都修。找到最大的。修好。继续。
4. Write it down or it didn't happen.写下来才算数。 If you can't explain it simply, you don't understand it yet.讲不清楚=你还没懂。
5. Your job changes.你的工作会变。 Week 1 = understand. Week 2 = people. Week 3 = systems. Week 4 = multiply. Don't skip ahead.第一周=理解。第二周=人。第三周=系统。第四周=复制。不要跳。
6. The test that matters:最重要的测试： If you disappear for 30 days, does each store get better, worse, or stay the same?如果你消失30天，每家店变好、变坏、还是不变？
