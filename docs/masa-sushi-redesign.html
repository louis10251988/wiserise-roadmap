Our Story

Why Greenville Chooses MASA

MASA Sushi Sake & Grill + Bar is one of the best Japanese restaurants in Greenville, North Carolina, located at 704 SE Greenville Blvd — just minutes from East Carolina University (ECU) and the Greenville Convention Center.

Our mission is simple: deliver an exceptional All-You-Can-Eat dining experience with the freshest sushi, sizzling hibachi, handcrafted signature rolls, and a full bar of cocktails and premium sake. Whether you're a student looking for affordable AYCE sushi, a family celebrating a special occasion, or an office manager planning a catering order, MASA has you covered.

What makes MASA stand out? Our chefs prepare every dish to order using fresh, high-quality ingredients. The atmosphere blends modern Japanese design with warm hospitality — think vibrant lanterns, a lively bar scene, and a welcoming team that treats every guest like family.

Location: 704 SE Greenville Blvd, Greenville, NC 27858 (near ECU, Greenville Mall, and I-264). Free parking available. Wheelchair accessible.
