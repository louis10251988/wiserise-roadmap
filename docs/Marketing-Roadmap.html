The first-principles answer

Start with the constraint that blocks revenue. Do not start with the loudest channel.

If guests do not trust us, they do not choose us. If they do not return, acquisition is wasted. Therefore: protect trust → create the next visit → amplify proof.

The revenue machine

01 · Earn choice

Trust

Google rating, complete listings, real photos, honest replies.

02 · Earn return

Visit 2

A clear next-visit reason, captured with consent and floor follow-through.

03 · Build habit

Visit 3

A simple bridge from first visit to regular guest. One message at a time.

04 · Amplify

Scale

Content, video, partnerships, then paid tests only when the base is healthy.

One system, three operators

Anson

Decides the work

Owns the weekly plan, brand choices, quality check, and handoffs.

Hermes

Moves the work

Reads Brain, drafts content and replies, tracks tickets, and reports gaps. Never auto-publishes.

GMs

Prove the work

Own the floor truth: listings, guest experience, approval, and store execution.

What this roadmap is not

✕ Not “post more” without a goal.   ✕ Not ads before trust.   ✕ Not a second marketing plan.   ✕ Not auto-publishing.   ✓ It is the timeline for standing up the confirmed system.

Red flags

Missing fact

Mark MISSING INPUT. Do not fill the gap with a guess.

Blocked approval

Show WHAT is waiting, WHO owns it, and WHEN it blocks the work.

Unknown result

Mark UNKNOWN. Add the measurement needed for the next decision.
